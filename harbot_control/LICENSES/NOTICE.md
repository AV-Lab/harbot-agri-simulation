# Source and license notice

`harbot_control` consolidates the retained controller configuration and launch
files from Robotnik's `rbkairos_control` 1.0.0 and `summit_xl_control` 1.1.3
packages. Both former manifests declared BSD, and both source trees carried the
same Robotnik BSD text reproduced in `Robotnik-BSD-3-Clause.txt`.

The repository-authored consolidation changes remain covered by the root MIT
license. The Robotnik copyright and BSD conditions continue to apply to the
retained upstream files.

Recorded upstream locations from the former manifests:

- <https://github.com/RobotnikAutomation/rbkairos_sim.git>
- <https://github.com/RobotnikAutomation/summit_xl_sim.git>

The former manifests attributed `rbkairos_control` to Roberto Guzmán and
`summit_xl_control` to Robotnik Automation's maintainers. Their Git history is
retained across this directory-only migration.
